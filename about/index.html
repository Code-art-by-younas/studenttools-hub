About StudentTools Hub

Free tools, built for students.

StudentTools Hub started with a simple frustration: every student calculator online was either buried in ads, demanded a signup, or looked like it was built in 2005.

So we built the alternative — a small set of genuinely useful academic tools that are free forever, work great on phones, and respect your privacy. Every calculation happens in your browser. We never see your marks, grades or dates.

What we believe

  • Usefulness first. We only publish tools students actually need.
  • No dark patterns. No fake download buttons, no forced signups, no misleading ads.
  • Learn, don't just calculate. Every tool explains its formula with a worked example.

Who builds this?

StudentTools Hub is an independent project by a software engineering student who got tired of bad calculator websites. New tools are added based on what students ask for — tell us what you need.

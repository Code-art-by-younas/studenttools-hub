Percentage Calculator

Three percentage tools in one: convert marks to percentage, find X percent of any number, and compute percentage increase or decrease.

–

How to use the percentage calculator

  1. Choose the calculation type from the dropdown.
  2. Marks → Percentage: enter obtained marks and total marks.
  3. X% of a number: enter the percentage and the number.
  4. Increase / decrease: enter the original and the new value.

Formula

Percentage = (Obtained ÷ Total) × 100
X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y
Change % = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Worked example

Example: You scored 425 out of 500. Percentage = (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%. A shop discount of 20% on Rs. 1500 = (20 ÷ 100) × 1500 = Rs. 300 off.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percentage from marks?

Divide your obtained marks by the total marks and multiply by 100. Example: 425 ÷ 500 × 100 = 85%.

How do I find X percent of a number?

Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply by the number. Example: 20% of 1500 = 0.20 × 1500 = 300.

How is percentage increase calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.

What is the difference between percent and percentage points?

A rise from 40% to 50% is a 10 percentage-point rise, but a 25% relative increase. They are often confused — now you know the difference.

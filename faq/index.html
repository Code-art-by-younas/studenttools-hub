Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our free student tools.

Are StudentTools Hub tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no signup, no account and no hidden charges — and we plan to keep it that way.

Do I need to sign up or log in?

No. Open any tool and start calculating immediately.

Will my marks or grades be stored anywhere?

No. All calculations run in your browser. We never see or store what you type.

Do the calculators work on mobile?

Yes — the site is mobile-first with large, touch-friendly inputs.

Which CGPA scale do you use?

The CGPA calculator works with whatever scale your transcript uses — just enter your semester GPAs as they appear. The GPA calculator uses the common 4.0 grade scale.

My university's grading is different. Will results match?

The math is standard, but institutions differ in details (A+ values, retake policies). Treat results as estimates and confirm with your official records.

How do I suggest a new tool?

Use our contact page — we build what students actually ask for.

Can I link to your calculators from my blog or class group?

Please do! Linking is encouraged.

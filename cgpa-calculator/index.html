How to use the cgpa calculator

  1. Add a row for every completed semester using + Add semester.
  2. Enter the GPA you earned that semester (e.g. 3.50).
  3. Enter the credit hours you took that semester (e.g. 18).
  4. Click Calculate CGPA to see your cumulative result instantly.

Formula

CGPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Hours
where Quality Points for a semester = Semester GPA × Credit Hours

Worked example

Example: Semester 1: GPA 3.50 × 18 credits = 63.0 points. Semester 2: GPA 3.20 × 15 credits = 48.0 points. CGPA = (63.0 + 48.0) ÷ (18 + 15) = 111 ÷ 33 = 3.36.

Frequently asked questions

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It is the weighted average of the GPAs you earned in every semester so far, where semesters with more credit hours count more.

How is CGPA calculated?

Multiply each semester's GPA by its credit hours to get quality points, add all quality points together, then divide by the total credit hours across all semesters.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA measures one semester (or term). CGPA is cumulative — it combines every semester you have completed into a single number.

What is considered a good CGPA?

On a 4.0 scale, 3.5 and above is generally considered strong, but expectations differ by university, country and field. Always check your own institution's standards.

Can my CGPA improve after a bad semester?

Yes. Because CGPA is a weighted average, strong performance in later semesters — especially ones with more credit hours — pulls it back up.

My university uses a 5.0 or 10.0 scale. Does this work?

The math is the same on any scale: enter your semester GPAs as they appear on your transcript and the calculator handles the rest.

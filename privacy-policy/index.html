Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Our approach in one sentence

We don't collect your calculator inputs. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your web browser (JavaScript). The numbers you type — marks, grades, dates — are processed on your device and never sent to our servers, because we don't operate any servers that receive them.

What we may collect

  • Contact form: if you message us, we receive your name, email and message so we can reply. We don't share or sell it.
  • Analytics (future): we may use privacy-friendly analytics (e.g. Cloudflare Web Analytics) or Google Analytics to understand aggregate usage (pages visited, device type). This data is aggregated, not linked to you personally.
  • Advertising (future): if we join Google AdSense, Google may use cookies to serve and measure ads. You can control ad personalization in your Google ad settings.

Cookies

We don't set any cookies ourselves today. Third-party services we add later (analytics, advertising) may set their own — this policy will be updated if so.

Third-party services

Our site is hosted on a static hosting provider and delivered via a CDN. These providers process standard technical data (like IP addresses) to deliver the site securely — we don't control or access it beyond aggregate statistics.

Children

Our tools are useful for school students, but we don't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above. Questions? Contact us.

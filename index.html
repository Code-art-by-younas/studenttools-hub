Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes — every tool on StudentTools Hub is free to use, with no signup, no account and no hidden charges.

Do I need to create an account?

No. All calculators work instantly in your browser.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Every calculation happens on your device. We don't see, store or transmit anything you type into a tool.

Do the tools work on mobile phones?

Yes — the site is designed mobile-first and every calculator is touch-friendly.

Are the results official?

Our calculators use standard formulas, but grading scales and policies differ by institution. Always confirm important results (like final CGPA) with your official transcript.

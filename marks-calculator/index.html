Marks Calculator

Add all your subjects with obtained and total marks to get subject-wise percentages, your overall percentage, totals and an indicative grade.

How to use the marks calculator

  1. Add a row per subject with + Add subject.
  2. Enter obtained marks and total marks for each.
  3. Click Calculate Marks for the full breakdown.

Formula

Subject % = (Obtained ÷ Total) × 100
Overall % = (Total Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Worked example

Example: Physics 85/100, Math 90/100, English 78/100. Total = 253/300. Overall = 84.33% (Grade A on the indicative scale).

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my overall percentage?

Add up all obtained marks, add up all total marks, then divide and multiply by 100. Heavier subjects (more marks) automatically count more.

Is the grade shown here official?

No — grading scales differ by board and university. The grade shown is indicative only; your institution's scale is what counts.

Can obtained marks exceed total marks?

No — the calculator will show an error, since that usually means a typo.

Does this work for any exam board?

Yes. It works with marks from any board, university or country because it only uses your numbers.

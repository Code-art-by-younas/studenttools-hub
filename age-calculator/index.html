Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months and days from your date of birth — plus total days lived and a countdown to your next birthday.

How to use the age calculator

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. Click Calculate Age.
  3. See your exact age, total days lived, and days until your next birthday.

Formula

Age is computed with real calendar math (varying month lengths and leap years), not by dividing days by 365.

Worked example

Example: Born 15 March 2004, today 3 October 2026 → 22 years, 6 months, 18 days old.

Frequently asked questions

How is exact age calculated?

By comparing year, month and day separately using the real calendar — accounting for months of different lengths and leap years.

Why not just divide days by 365?

Because years aren't exactly 365 days and months aren't equal. Calendar math gives the age you'd actually state on a form.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes — including 29 February birthdays.

Is my date of birth sent anywhere?

No. Like all our tools, this calculator runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored.

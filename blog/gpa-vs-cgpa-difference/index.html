GPA vs CGPA: What's the Difference?

Published 2026-10-03

Students mix these up constantly — even final-year ones. Let's settle it in two minutes.

GPA: one semester

GPA (Grade Point Average) measures a single semester or term. It answers: "How did I do this semester?"

CGPA: everything so far

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) combines all your semesters into one number. It answers: "How have I done overall?"

How they connect

Your CGPA is the credit-weighted average of your semester GPAs. That's it — CGPA is just all your GPAs blended together.

Semester GPAs: 3.2 (15 credits), 3.6 (18 credits), 3.4 (16 credits).
CGPA = (3.2×15 + 3.6×18 + 3.4×16) ÷ 49 = 3.41

Which one do employers and universities ask for?

Almost always CGPA — it's the headline number on your transcript. GPA matters most while a semester is in progress, or when a scholarship has a per-semester requirement.

Quick answers

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA measures a single semester; CGPA is the cumulative, credit-weighted average of all semesters completed.

Which is more important, GPA or CGPA?

CGPA is the headline number on transcripts and what employers/graduate schools usually ask for.

How to use the gpa calculator

  1. Add a row per subject with + Add subject.
  2. Type the subject name (optional) and its credit hours.
  3. Choose your grade (A+, A, B+ … F) from the dropdown.
  4. Click Calculate GPA for your weighted semester GPA.

Formula

GPA = Total Quality Points ÷ Total Credit Hours
where Quality Points for a subject = Grade Points × Credit Hours

Worked example

Example: Calculus: A (4.0) × 3 credits = 12.0. Physics: B+ (3.3) × 4 credits = 13.2. English: A− (3.7) × 3 credits = 11.1. GPA = (12.0 + 13.2 + 11.1) ÷ 10 = 3.63.

Frequently asked questions

What is GPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is the weighted average of your grades in a single semester or term, weighted by each subject's credit hours.

What grade points do you use?

A standard 4.0 scale: A+/A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C− = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, F = 0.0.

Will this exactly match my university's GPA?

Most universities use a 4.0 scale like this one, but some differ (for example in how they treat A+ or retakes). Treat the result as an estimate and confirm with your official transcript.

Do failed subjects count?

Yes — an F contributes 0 grade points but its credit hours still count in the total, which is why failures pull GPA down sharply.

GPA vs CGPA — which do employers ask for?

Usually CGPA (cumulative). Use this GPA calculator per semester, then combine semesters with our CGPA calculator.
